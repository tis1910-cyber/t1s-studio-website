THE T1S MANIFESTO

Keep something
real.

A simple promise for a channel being built in a world where “real” is becoming harder to identify at a glance.

01

Real world.

The subject comes first. A field, an animal, a bird, a road, weather, a quiet room outdoors — whatever is actually there.

02

Real footage.

No generated animal is substituted for the one that never appeared. No synthetic landscape replaces a boring sky. If the camera did not see it, the video does not pretend it did.

03

Real sound.

Natural sound is the default. Music can be an optional version when it suits the audience, but the real-world recording remains the foundation.

04

Honest thumbnails.

A thumbnail should come from the video itself. It may be cropped, brightened, balanced or labelled — but not rebuilt into a scene that never happened.

05

Slow observation.

Not every second needs a cut. Not every minute needs a payoff. Sometimes the interesting part is learning to notice the micro-events.

06

No fake drama.

No invented tension, staged danger or manufactured spectacle just to force attention. The real moment is allowed to be enough.

07

AI can stay behind the camera.

Modern tools may help with analysis, writing, planning or the website. They do not replace the reality presented to the viewer.

08

Appreciate the small moments.

A head turns. Rain starts. A bird lands. A cow walks toward the lens. Nothing “big” happened — and that is often exactly the point.

That is the whole idea.

Real world first.
