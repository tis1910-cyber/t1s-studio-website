04 / WHAT IS SLOW TV?

Not nothing.
Micro-events.

I discovered Slow TV while filming and publishing T1S, and it immediately made sense to me. It does not need a cut every second. It can be calm, observational and almost uneventful — until you start noticing the tiny things.

The videos can be watched actively, left on a television, used as ambience, or simply treated as a small window into somewhere else. There is no prescribed way to enjoy them.

06 / THE STORY

APR2026T1S Studio begins

It started with a camera —
and a little frustration.

I have always enjoyed photography, and later video. T1S Studio began in April 2026 as a way to push that hobby further, learn the craft at a higher level and keep exploring what I enjoy filming.

At the same time, I was becoming frustrated by how much generated content was appearing online. When I tried to find simple, real, non-generated content for my child, it was surprisingly difficult. I suspected I probably was not the only person looking for something more genuine.

Then I discovered ambience, relaxation videos and Slow TV. I realised I actually loved the format: a camera that can stay still, moments that do not need to perform, and enough time to notice what would normally pass unnoticed.

Most filming currently happens in England because that is where I live, but T1S is not meant to be geographically boxed in. If I travel and find something worth recording, the camera can come too.

For now, I prefer to stay behind the camera. The real world is the subject.
